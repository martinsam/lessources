# Our Ecological Impact


## 7.5 hectares without synthetic chemicals

{{< figure src="images/brouette.jpeg" title="Wheelbarrow at rest" size="300x" >}}

We took the bold step of settling on 7.5 hectares of land and cultivating it mainly with the help of our animals, plants, and our own hands. Since 2016, in the Creuse region, we have been a family-run farm, a small alternative operation producing in a traditional way, without the use of chemicals.

We have chosen an agriculture that respects the soil, living organisms, and the seasons. Here, each plot is designed as an ecosystem: hedges protect the crops, trees provide shade and biodiversity, and animals naturally contribute to soil fertility. Nothing is left to chance, yet everything is done with patience.

## Biodiversity and soil regeneration

{{< figure src="images/plan_aerien.jpg" title="Aerial view" size="600x" >}}

We cultivate by following natural rhythms, favoring diversity over monoculture, and quality over quantity. Our practices are inspired by the common-sense farming of the past, enriched by current knowledge in agroecology. We aim to regenerate the soil rather than exhaust it, to pass on land that is more alive than what we received.

## Understanding living systems

{{< figure src="images/cochons.jpeg" title="3 little pigs" size="600x" >}}

Our farm is also a place of learning and sharing. We welcome those who wish to understand where their food comes from, reconnect with the cycle of the seasons, or simply discover another way of producing and consuming.

Choosing our farm means supporting human-scale, local, and transparent agriculture. It means encouraging a model that puts living systems back at the heart of decisions. It means believing that, at our scale, step by step, we can cultivate much more than products: we can cultivate connection, meaning, and hope.

And what if the footprint we leave behind also became a seed for the future? 🌱
