We would be delighted to welcome your group (10 people or more) for a visit of our garden, answer your questions, and share the story of our little corner of paradise. A tasting of our farm products is included. If you are accompanied by children, we will be happy to take them for a pony and donkey ride through the garden, and extend the visit with a special moment feeding the animals in their enclosures.

Another option, by reservation: a workshop at the farm on a topic of your choice.
